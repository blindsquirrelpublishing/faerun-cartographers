# Prompt pattern

Fill the fields from the current source. Do not leave placeholders in the image request.

> Redraw the attached geographic source for [PLACE] as an earnest ten-year-old dungeon master's homemade game map from 1981. Show the flat paper drawing itself, mostly grey school pencil on faint square graph paper, with a little dark blue ballpoint correction, uneven blue colored-pencil water, green gardens and occasional tan roofs. Make it look like hours of careful child effort: shaky circles, retraced walls, tiny simple top-down homes, awkward ruler streets, hand-lettered title, modest smudges and light eraser traces. Keep it readable. No polished illustration, CAD frame, digital fonts, professional calligraphy, isometric view, props or people.
>
> GEOGRAPHY SOURCE: [identify attachment]. STYLE REFERENCE: [identify sample if attached; marks only, never its geography]. Preserve [source extent and north], [land/water connections], [roads and bridge endpoints], [wall circuits and gates], [gardens and their walls], [landmark positions and relative order], and [open spaces and district building masses]. Simplify roof detail only within the source blocks. Do not move sites, connect separate waters, add crossings, or make mainland into an island.
>
> LABELS: [verified title and a few source names]. Handwrite labels in spare space and use a small homemade key. [Include supported scale tied to the actual graph spacing, or omit numeric scale.] [Include a date only when requested.] Keep all graph cells square and the entire geographic extent visible.

## Sample limits

`assets/example.jpg` shows Mirabar, made during the style's first use. It is an approximate image conversion. Use its paper, pencil, color and handwriting as a finish reference, not as a survey or a universal town layout. The handwritten scale in this sample has not been checked and must not be reused as scale evidence.
